    Individual dares

    Each walker has their own list and does whatever tops it. Your donation goes to that walker's JustGiving page.

      How it works

      1. Suggest a dare for the whole group or for one walker. We read every suggestion before it goes on the list.
      2. Vote for group dares for free, with one vote per dare.
      3. Donate to a walker's dare on JustGiving, put the dare's code in your donation message, then log it here.
      4. We match it up against JustGiving and the dare climbs that walker's list.
      5. The top dares happen on the walk.

      The small print

      You donate on JustGiving, so your money goes to the charity whichever dare wins. This site never touches it.

      We can turn down any dare that's unsafe, illegal or bad for the moor, even if it's winning. The next one down takes its place.

      If two dares tie, the one suggested first ranks higher.

      Donations are handled by JustGiving.
